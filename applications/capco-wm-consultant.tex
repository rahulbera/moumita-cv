I am applying for the Wealth Management Consultant role with KYC/AML review
experience. I am an AML, KYC and financial crime compliance professional with
nearly nine years in banking, based in Zurich and holding a Swiss B permit, so
no sponsorship is required.

The first assignment you describe -- a large-scale KYC/AML review and
remediation programme -- is the work I have done for most of my career rather
than a project I would be learning on. At IndusInd Bank I ran KYC verification,
remediation and periodic refresh for High Net Worth, affluent and corporate
clients: reviewing client files against requirements, recording deficiencies,
obtaining Source of Funds and Source of Wealth documentation, and driving files
through to completion. Structured file reviews and process improvements on that
work reduced documentation errors by 25\%, and at HDFC Bank cross-functional
work on onboarding cut turnaround time by 20\%. Handling volume without losing
accuracy is the discipline the job asks for, and those are the two numbers I
have for it.

On higher-risk files I have applied a risk-based approach to identify cases
requiring enhanced review, conducted PEP, sanctions and adverse media
screening, and verified Ultimate Beneficial Ownership for legal entities
including LLPs and private trusts -- which is where I have met wealth
structuring in practice. Where a case was non-standard, I escalated it to
Compliance, Operations or Internal Audit with the file in a state they could
act on. Working alongside client advisers and front teams was constant: I
guided branch colleagues on onboarding standards and documentation quality, and
much of the job was persuading people to produce documents they had not
prioritised.

I have built my Swiss regulatory grounding deliberately since relocating --
SAQ-recognised AMLA/AMLO-FINMA 2025 training and the Basel Institute on
Governance course on combating terrorism financing -- and my German is at B1.
Consulting is a deliberate step for me: after nearly nine years inside three
banks, I want to bring that view to remediation work across institutions rather
than one at a time, and Capco's focus on Risk \& Compliance in Swiss wealth and
asset management is where my experience actually sits.

I would welcome the chance to discuss the assignment in more detail.
