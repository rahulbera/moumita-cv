I am applying for the Client Onboarding \& Documentation Specialist role in
Zurich. I am an AML, KYC and financial crime compliance professional with
nearly nine years in banking, based in Zurich and holding a Swiss B permit, so
no sponsorship is required.

Client onboarding and the documentation behind it is what I have done
throughout my career. As Chief Manager at IndusInd Bank I led end-to-end
onboarding and periodic KYC reviews for High Net Worth, affluent and corporate
clients: obtaining Source of Funds and Source of Wealth documentation,
identifying what was missing, and closing the gaps with the client and the
internal teams holding the relationship. That coordination is the part your
role describes with Sales, and it is the part I am used to owning. Documentation
rarely arrives complete; getting it there is the job.

On the AML/CFT side I have run PEP, sanctions and adverse media screening,
verified Ultimate Beneficial Ownership for legal entities including LLPs and
private trusts, managed Customer Identification Program activities, applied a
risk-based approach to identify cases requiring enhanced review, and escalated
concerns to Compliance, Operations and Internal Audit. I have planned and
completed periodic reviews and KYC remediation, maintained audit-ready client
records and supported internal compliance reviews, including answering the
queries that come with them. In my most recent role, structured KYC file
reviews and process improvements reduced documentation errors by 25\%.

Since relocating to Switzerland I have built my regulatory grounding here
deliberately: SAQ-recognised AMLA/AMLO-FINMA 2025 training on the prevention of
money laundering, and the Basel Institute on Governance course on combating
terrorism financing, which speaks directly to the CFT half of this role,
alongside certifications in risk management and in AML/KYC for banking and
insurance. Having worked under RBI regulation and trained on the Swiss
framework, I am comfortable moving between regulatory environments. I work in
English and hold German at B1.

I should be straightforward about one thing: my degrees are in Linguistics and
English Literature rather than law or finance. My compliance grounding is
certified rather than academic, and it is above all practised, across three
banks and nearly nine years. What draws me to this role is the chance to apply
that in a fintech and to learn the mechanics of the structured investment
product value chain from the inside.

I would welcome the chance to discuss how that experience fits your team.
