I am writing to apply for the Anti-Money Laundering Analyst position at
CoinFlip.

Investigating suspicious activity and assessing financial crime risk is the
work I have done for nearly nine years in banking, most recently as Chief
Manager at IndusInd Bank. I have conducted PEP, sanctions and adverse media
screening, applied a risk-based approach to identify accounts and transactions
warranting enhanced review, verified Ultimate Beneficial Ownership for legal
entities including limited liability partnerships and private trusts, and
escalated cases to Compliance, Operations and Internal Audit where activity or
documentation required further assessment. Handling customer-reported
unauthorised and suspicious transactions, and working with fraud teams on
disputes and chargebacks, formed a regular part of that work.

Examining files and data to confirm that AML requirements are met is the other
half of it. I have run KYC verification, remediation and periodic review,
obtained Source of Funds and Source of Wealth documentation, and handled
FATCA and CRS requirements. I have also maintained audit-ready records and
supported internal compliance reviews, including responding to the queries
those reviews generate. In my most recent role, structured file reviews and
process improvements reduced documentation errors by 25 per cent, and
cross-functional work on onboarding at HDFC Bank cut turnaround time by 20 per
cent -- the kind of measurable risk-mitigation reporting your role calls for.

My regulatory grounding is current and certified: SAQ-recognised
AMLA/AMLO-FINMA 2025 training on the prevention of money laundering, the Basel
Institute on Governance course on combating terrorism financing, and further
certifications in risk management and in AML/KYC for banking and insurance. I
have been extending that grounding into crypto AML, digital assets and virtual
asset compliance, which is what draws me to CoinFlip specifically: the
underlying obligations are the ones I know well, applied to an asset class and
a delivery model where the typologies are still taking shape.

I work daily in Microsoft Excel and Word, am fluent in business English, and
also speak Hindi and Bengali.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss my application and thank you for
your consideration.
