I am writing to apply for the position of Relationship Manager, Global South
Asia \& Africa, within Deutsche Bank Private Bank. I hold a Swiss B permit,
which entitles me to work in Switzerland without any requirement for employer
sponsorship.

My background combines two elements central to this mandate. The first is
Non-Resident Indian banking. I have managed NRI client relationships and worked
with the account structures and regulatory requirements particular to them,
including NRE, NRO and FCNR accounts, repatriation and the applicable
FEMA requirements. Understanding where a client is resident, how funds may move
across borders and which rules govern that movement is not peripheral to this
market; it is the foundation of the client conversation.

The second is client acquisition. I have carried acquisition and asset targets
alongside my relationship responsibilities, sourcing new clients and developing
existing relationships rather than servicing a static book. Over nearly nine
years at Axis Bank, HDFC Bank and most recently as Chief Manager at IndusInd
Bank, I have managed High Net Worth, affluent and corporate relationships across
deposits, lending, investments, insurance, foreign exchange and remittances,
working with the product specialists involved in each.

I would draw particular attention to the accountability you describe for the
Anti-Money Laundering aspects of the relationship. Client onboarding, Know Your
Client, source of funds and source of wealth, PEP and sanctions screening,
Ultimate Beneficial Ownership for legal entities including trusts, and the
judgement of when account behaviour warrants escalation are areas I have worked
in directly and have continued to study. I hold SAQ-recognised AMLA/AMLO-FINMA
2025 certification and have completed the Basel Institute on Governance course
on combating terrorism financing. An Accountable Client Owner who understands
these obligations from the inside is, I would suggest, of some value to the
franchise.

I am fluent in business English, speak Hindi and Bengali, and hold German at B1
level, which I am continuing to develop.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss my application with you and thank
you for your consideration.
