I am writing to apply for the position of Client Documentation Generalist
(100\%) in Zurich. I hold a Swiss B permit, which entitles me to work in
Switzerland without any requirement for employer sponsorship.

Reviewing client documentation for completeness and accuracy has been the
substance of my work throughout nearly nine years in banking. At IndusInd Bank
I ran KYC verification, remediation and periodic refresh, reviewing files for
both individuals and legal entities, recording the deficiencies identified, and
requesting the missing documents from clients and from the teams holding the
relationship. In my most recent role, structured file reviews and process
improvements reduced documentation errors by 25 per cent. Supporting the front
on questions of documentation standards is equally familiar: I advised branch
colleagues on onboarding standards and documentation quality.

For legal entities I have verified Ultimate Beneficial Ownership, including
limited liability partnerships and private trusts, and reviewed corporate
registration records as part of the identification process. I have handled
FATCA and CRS requirements at onboarding and throughout the relationship. A
substantial part of my work has involved Non-Resident Indian clients, where
residency status determines the permissible account structure, the
documentation required and the cross-border treatment of funds; that experience
bears directly on reviewing tax formalities for consistency. I have also
managed account opening, maintenance and closure across the client lifecycle,
keeping records audit-ready and supporting internal compliance reviews, much of
it for High Net Worth and affluent clients.

Two points on the formal profile should be stated plainly. I have not worked
under CDB 20, and my academic background is in Linguistics and English
Literature rather than a commercial apprenticeship. Your posting asks for three
years of experience in bank customer documentation; I have nearly nine, across
three banks. I have also built my Swiss regulatory grounding on my own
initiative, completing SAQ-recognised AMLA/AMLO-FINMA 2025 training and the
Basel Institute on Governance course on combating terrorism financing, and
would approach the Code of Conduct in the same way. My German is at B1 level.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss my application with you and thank
you for your consideration.
