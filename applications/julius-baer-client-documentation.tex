of what I have done throughout my career. At IndusInd Bank I ran KYC
verification, remediation and periodic refresh: reviewing files for individuals
and legal entities, recording what was deficient, and going back to the client
and to the teams holding the relationship for what was missing. Structured file

25\%. Requesting missing documents from the front and answering their questions
on documentation standards is familiar ground -- I guided branch teams on
precisely that.

For legal entities I have verified Ultimate Beneficial Ownership, including
LLPs and private trusts, and checked corporate registration records as part of
the identification process. I have handled FATCA and CRS requirements at
onboarding and throughout the relationship, and managed account opening,
maintenance and closure across the client lifecycle, keeping records
audit-ready and supporting internal compliance reviews. Much of that work was
for High Net Worth and affluent clients, where discretion is not optional.

CDB 20, and my formal education is in Linguistics and English Literature rather
than a commercial apprenticeship. Against that: your posting asks for three
years in bank customer documentation and I have nearly nine, across three
banks. And when I needed the Swiss regulatory framework, I went and learned it
-- SAQ-recognised AMLA/AMLO-FINMA 2025 training and the Basel Institute on
Governance course on combating terrorism financing, both on my own initiative.
I would come to the Code of Conduct the same way. My German is at B1.

I would welcome the chance to discuss how that experience fits your team.
