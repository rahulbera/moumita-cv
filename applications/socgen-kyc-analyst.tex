I am writing to apply for the position of Intern KYC Analyst within the Zurich
KYC Hub, reference 26000K6Y.

Having spent nearly nine years in banking, most recently as Chief Manager at
IndusInd Bank, I am now seeking to establish my career within the Swiss
financial sector. This position is of particular interest to me as it combines
client onboarding and periodic review, in which I am experienced, with exposure
to the Swiss and German regulatory environment, which I would value the
opportunity to develop further. I hold a Swiss B permit, which entitles me to
work in Switzerland without any requirement for employer sponsorship.

Throughout my career, I have been responsible for client onboarding and periodic
review for High Net Worth, affluent and corporate clients. This work has
included conducting due diligence on clients and associated parties, verifying
Ultimate Beneficial Ownership for legal entities including limited liability
partnerships and private trusts, reviewing director and corporate registration
records, and carrying out PEP, sanctions and adverse media screening. I have
applied a risk-based approach in assessing files and documenting a formalised
opinion, identified documentation deficiencies, and escalated matters requiring
further compliance assessment. Maintaining accurate client profiles within core
banking and CRM systems formed part of my daily responsibilities. In my most
recent role, structured file reviews and process improvements reduced
documentation errors by 25 per cent.

I am equally accustomed to acting as a point of contact between business lines,
Compliance and management. In previous positions, I advised branch colleagues on
onboarding standards and documentation quality, and worked closely with
Operations, Compliance and Internal Audit to resolve cases requiring input from
several teams.

My academic background is in Linguistics and English Literature, which I have
since complemented with regulatory training directed specifically at this
market: SAQ-recognised AMLA/AMLO-FINMA 2025 training on the prevention of money
laundering, and the Basel Institute on Governance course on combating terrorism
financing. I am fluent in business English, hold German at B1 level and am
continuing to develop it, and additionally speak Hindi and Bengali.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss my application with you and thank
you for your consideration.
