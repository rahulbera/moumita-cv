I am writing to apply for the position of Intern KYC Analyst within the Zurich
KYC Hub, reference 26000K6Y.

Having spent nearly nine years in banking, most recently as Chief Manager at
IndusInd Bank, I am now seeking to establish my career within the Swiss
financial sector. This programme is of particular interest to me because it
combines client onboarding and periodic review work, in which I am experienced,
with exposure to the Swiss and German regulatory environment, which I am keen
to develop further.

Throughout my career I have managed client onboarding and periodic review for
High Net Worth, affluent and corporate clients. This has included performing
due diligence on clients and associated parties, verifying Ultimate Beneficial
Ownership for legal entities including limited liability partnerships and
private trusts, reviewing directors and corporate registration records, and
conducting PEP, sanctions and adverse media screening. I have applied a
risk-based approach when assessing and documenting my view on a file,
identified documentation deficiencies, and escalated matters requiring further
compliance assessment. Maintaining accurate client profiles within core banking
and CRM systems formed part of my daily responsibilities, and structured file
reviews together with process improvements reduced documentation errors by 25\%
in my most recent role.

I am also accustomed to acting as a point of contact between business lines,
Compliance and management. In previous roles I advised branch colleagues on
onboarding standards and documentation quality, and worked closely with
Operations, Compliance and Internal Audit to resolve cases requiring input from
several teams.

My academic background is in Linguistics and English Literature. I have since
complemented this with regulatory training directed specifically at this
market: SAQ-recognised AMLA/AMLO-FINMA 2025 training on the prevention of money
laundering, and the Basel Institute on Governance course on combating terrorism
financing. I work in English, hold German at B1 level and continue to study it,
and also speak Hindi and Bengali.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss my application with you, and thank
you for your consideration.
