I am applying for the Intern KYC Analyst position in the Zurich KYC Hub,
reference 26000K6Y. You will see from my CV that I have nearly nine years in
banking and last held the title of Chief Manager, so let me be direct about why
I am applying at this level. My experience is Indian banking; what I do not yet
have is Swiss regulatory practice. An eighteen-month posting in a Swiss KYC Hub
covering two branches is a serious way to acquire it, and I would rather build
that foundation properly than claim a seniority I have not yet earned in this
market.

What I would bring on day one is the work itself. I have run client onboarding
and periodic review for High Net Worth, affluent and corporate clients:
performing due diligence on the client and on associated parties, verifying
Ultimate Beneficial Ownership for legal entities including LLPs and private
trusts, checking directors and corporate registration records, and conducting
PEP, sanctions and adverse media screening. I have applied a risk-based
approach to form and document a view on a file, flagged documentation issues,
and escalated cases that needed Compliance to look again. Maintaining client
profiles in core banking and CRM systems was daily work. Structured file
reviews and process improvements reduced documentation errors by 25\% in my
last role.

Acting as a point of contact between business lines, Compliance and management
is also familiar: I guided branch colleagues on onboarding standards and
documentation quality, and coordinated with Operations, Compliance and Internal
Audit to close cases that could not be resolved in one place.

On the formal profile, I should be straightforward: my degrees are in
Linguistics and English Literature rather than finance, economics or law. I
have built the regulatory grounding since, and deliberately for this market --
SAQ-recognised AMLA/AMLO-FINMA 2025 training and the Basel Institute on
Governance course on combating terrorism financing. I work in English, hold
German at B1 and am continuing with it, and also speak Hindi and Bengali.

I would welcome the chance to discuss the role.
